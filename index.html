Different questions for different work

Trade by trade

A plasterer and a roofer need different things from the same phone call. These are starting points for what it might ask, and you can change any of them.

Builders

What the work is, whether drawings exist, the address and when they hope to start. Enough to tell an extension from a garden wall.

Roofers

Whether water is coming in now or it can wait, the type of roof, the address and how easy it is to get a ladder or scaffold up.

Plasterers

Which rooms and roughly how big, skim or full plaster, and whether the walls are ready or the room still has furniture in it.

Landscapers

Patio, fencing, turf or planting, the rough size of the garden, access for materials and any budget the customer mentions.

Heating engineers

The boiler or system involved, whether there is heating and hot water right now, and the address it all needs to happen at.

Carpenters

Doors, stairs, kitchens or bespoke work, any measurements they already have, and whether it sits inside a bigger build.

Straight answers

What tradesmen ask before they sign up

Will customers hate talking to AI?

Some would rather speak to a person, and it will not win everybody over. It tells them straight away that it is an AI, keeps things short and gets the details to you so you can ring back. The alternative at that moment is usually voicemail.

Can it tell a real job from a sales call?

It asks what the call is about and what needs doing, which exposes most sales calls quickly. You say what counts as a time-waster, and anything doubtful still reaches you by text.

What does it cost compared with a person answering?

Antek can only speak for its own prices: from £497 to set up and from £97 a month. Weigh that against whatever you pay now and what a lost job is worth to you.

Can it text me straight away?

Yes. Once the call ends you get a text with the job, the address and the timescale, ready for you to decide whether to ring back.

I only want it out of hours, is that possible?

Yes. Give Andy the hours you want it on and calls are forwarded to it during those hours alone. The rest of the time your phone rings as normal.

Is this the same as missed call text back?

No. Missed call text back sends a message after you have missed someone. This takes the call, has the conversation and leaves the customer knowing somebody has their details.

What if I am a plumber or an electrician?

Those trades have their own sites, so have a look at AI for plumbers or voice agents for electricians on call-outs.
